Useful OpenSSL Commands

Reference commands for certificate validation and troubleshooting.

Verifying CRL

openssl verify -crl_check_all -CAfile root.pem -untrusted intermediate.pem -CRLfile crl-bundle.pem certificate-to-verify.pem
openssl verify -crl_check_all -CAfile full-including-crl-bundle.pem certificate-to-verify.pem
openssl verify -crl_download -crl_check_all -CAfile root.pem -untrusted intermediate.pem certificate-to-verify.pem

Inspecting Certificates

openssl x509 -in certificate-to-inspect.pem -text -noout
openssl x509 -in certificate-to-inspect.pem -noout -dates
openssl x509 -in certificate-to-inspect.pem -noout -subject -issuer -serial

Inspecting CRL

openssl crl -in crl-to-inspect.pem -text -noout
