OpenSSL Revocation Check

Use the following OpenSSL command to verify that an end-user certificate is not revoked: 

openssl verify -crl_check -CAfile root.pem -CRLfile chain.crl chain.pem
  • root.pem is the root certificate.
  • chain.crl is the CRL bundle for the root and intermediate certificates.
  • chain.pem is the certificate chain containing the intermediate and end-user certificates.
